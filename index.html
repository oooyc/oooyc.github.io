About

I am a master's student in Control Science and Engineering at Wuhan University, advised by Prof. Jun Zhang, and a remote research intern at the GLINT Lab, Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Wenya Wang. I received my B.S. in Automation from Wuhan University in 2024.

My research interests are agentic systems (multi-agent systems, self-evolving agents, recursive self-improvement and agentic memory) and reinforcement learning for LLM agents (credit assignment, multi-agent RL and agentic RL). I am also interested in representation learning, mainly contrastive learning and self-supervised pre-training.

I am looking for Ph.D. positions starting in Fall 2027.
